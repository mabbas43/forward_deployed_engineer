# Travel and Expense Reimbursement

## Submitting expenses
Submit expense reports in Personio within 30 days of the expense, with photos of all receipts. Reports
approved by your manager by the 20th of the month are paid with that month's salary.

## Limits
Hotel: up to 150 EUR per night in Germany. Meals are reimbursed via the statutory per-diem rates.
Train travel: 2nd class; 1st class for journeys over four hours.
