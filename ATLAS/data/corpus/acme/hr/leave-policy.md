# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
| Employment type | Vacation days per year |
|---|---|
| Full-time (40 h) | 30 days |
| Part-time | pro rata to contracted hours |
| Apprentices | 30 days |
| Working students | 24 days |

Leave is requested in Personio at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.
Unused leave can be carried over until 31 March of the following year.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager before your shift starts. A doctor's certificate (eAU) is required from the fourth day
of illness; it is transmitted electronically by the health insurer.

## Special leave
Two days for your own wedding, one day for the birth of a child (partner), two days for the death of a close
family member, one day for moving house.
