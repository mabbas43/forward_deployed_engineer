# Parental Leave (Elternzeit)

## Requesting parental leave
Parental leave must be requested in writing at least seven weeks before it starts. HR confirms the dates in
writing within two weeks. Questions about Elterngeld are answered by the Elterngeldstelle, not by HR.

## Returning to work
Employees can return part-time during parental leave (up to 32 hours per week). Discuss the arrangement with
your manager and HR at least two months before the planned return.
