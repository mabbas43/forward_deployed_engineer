# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup

## Registering the authenticator app
Install Microsoft Authenticator from the App Store or Google Play. Go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo,
choose "Add sign-in method", select Authenticator app and scan the QR code.

## New phone
When you change phones, register the new phone at https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo before wiping the old one.
If the old phone is already gone, the IT Service Desk must reset your MFA registration after verifying your
identity via a video call with your company ID. MFA resets are always logged and the user's manager is informed.

## Hardware keys
Privileged accounts must use a FIDO2 security key. Keys are issued by IT on request; a lost key must be
reported within 24 hours.
