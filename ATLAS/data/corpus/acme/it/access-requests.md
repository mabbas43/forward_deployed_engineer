# Access Requests and Shared Drives

## Requesting access
Access to shared drives, SAP roles and applications is requested through the Service Portal under
"Request access". Every request needs approval from the data owner. Finance drives are owned by the Head of
Finance; HR drives by the HR Director.

## Approval times
Standard requests are processed within two business days after approval. Access for new joiners is created
automatically from the HR onboarding record on their first day.

## Access reviews
Data owners review access to their systems every quarter. Access not confirmed in the review is removed.
