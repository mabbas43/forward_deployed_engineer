# Password Policy (Draft, 2023)

## Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 8 characters and changed every 90 days. MFA is recommended but optional for
office-based staff.

## Account lockout
Accounts lock after 3 failed attempts and must be unlocked by the IT Service Desk.
