# Supplier Invoices and Payments

## Sending invoices
Suppliers send invoices to invoices@acme-logistics.de as PDF or XRechnung. Each invoice must reference a
purchase order (PO) number; invoices without a PO are returned.

## Duplicate charges
If a supplier invoice was paid twice, do not contact the bank. Open a billing ticket with both payment
references; Finance requests a credit note from the supplier and offsets it against the next invoice.
Refunds are processed within 10 business days after the credit note arrives.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days net. Early-payment discounts are taken automatically by Finance.
