# Job roles this course prepares you for

Straight answer, sorted by how well the course fits.

## Apply with confidence (the course maps 1:1)

**AI Engineer / GenAI Engineer / LLM Engineer** — the bulk of listings. Phases 1–3 are the job. With ~1 year at MedVance plus ATLAS, target *mid-level*, not junior. Don't apply for "Senior" yet unless the company is small — you'll get filtered, and it wastes a shot.

**AI Agent Engineer / Agentic AI Engineer** — Phases 3–5. Your strongest fit; you already had this title. Parloa, Cognigy, and n8n hire exactly this. The Phase 5 improvement curve is your differentiator.

**LLMOps Engineer / AI Platform Engineer** — Phases 7–8. Fewer listings, less competition, and evals expertise is rare. Your CI-gated evals + registry + online monitoring story is what these interviews probe.

**AI Solutions Engineer / Forward Deployed Engineer** — customer-facing builder roles at AI product companies (deepset, Parloa, Aleph Alpha run these). Needs all phases plus the ability to explain them; the write-ups prove that. Often the easiest door into a Series A/B company for someone remote.

## Apply with a specific angle

**AI Engineer at regulated companies** (banks, insurers, health, automotive, public sector) — Phase 6 is the ticket. Lead with GOVERNANCE.md and the AI Act posture. Fewer candidates can talk about this than can wire a RAG pipeline.

**AI Consultant / Freelancer (Freiberufler)** — the three sellable units are RAG (P2), MCP integrations (P4), governance (P6). This matches your remote-first plan and the visa path you researched.

**Backend Engineer, AI-focused** — if the pure AI roles don't land, Phase 8 makes you a credible Python backend hire at an AI company. Often the pragmatic entry.

## Don't apply yet (the course doesn't cover it)

**ML Engineer / Research Engineer** — they want training, fine-tuning, PEFT, model serving (vLLM/Triton), and math. ATLAS deliberately skips weights. Your roadmap lists these; they're a *second* course, after this one.

**AI Architect / Staff** — needs years of production scars, not one project.

## How to search

Titles vary. Search German boards for: *KI-Ingenieur*, *LLM Engineer*, *GenAI Engineer*, *AI Engineer (m/w/d)*, *Agentic AI*, *LLMOps*, *MLOps Engineer (LLM)*. Filter on stack keywords — LangGraph, RAG, MCP, evaluation, Langfuse/LangSmith — rather than title.

One honest note: the course makes you *credible* for these roles; the write-ups and the demo video are what get you *seen*. Without them, ATLAS is a repo nobody opens.
